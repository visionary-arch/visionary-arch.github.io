Privacy Policy — GrimCarnation: Idle Necro School

Package ID: com.grimcarnation.necroschool • Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Offline Single-Player Design & Local Save Data

GrimCarnation: Idle Necro School stores your campuses, classroom upgrades, Necromancer roster, equipment inventory, Grimoire research, and audio/display preferences locally on your device using browser storage and Android preferences. You may export or import a JSON backup anytime inside Settings.

2. Personal Information

The developer does not directly collect, store on external servers, or sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera feed, microphone audio, or precise location.

3. In-App Purchases & Optional Rewarded Video Ads

  • Zero Forced Ads: GrimCarnation: Idle Necro School contains zero banner ads and zero unprompted interstitial ads.
  • Google Play Billing: Optional store packs and upgrades are processed directly by Google Play. Payment credentials are handled exclusively by Google Play and are never shared with the application.
  • Optional Rewarded Ads: When you choose to watch an optional rewarded video ad (such as 2x Tuition Surge, 2x Offline Vault, or bonus event claims), Google AdMob may process standard device/advertising identifiers, coarse IP region, and ad-interaction diagnostics in accordance with your regional consent choices (Google User Messaging Platform) and Google's Privacy Policy. Where required by regional privacy regulations, you can review or update your consent choices inside Settings → Privacy & Ad Consent.

4. Children's Privacy

GrimCarnation: Idle Necro School does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Managing or Deleting Your Local Save Data

You can erase all local progress inside Settings → Reset Save, or uninstall the application at any time to remove locally stored data from your device.
