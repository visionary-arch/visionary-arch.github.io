Privacy Policy — Potion & Pawn: Idle Outpost

Package ID: com.potionandpawn.game • Effective Date: November 6, 2026

1. Offline Single-Player Design & Local Save Data

Potion & Pawn: Idle Outpost is a paid upfront, offline single-player shopkeeping idle game. Your game progress, unlocked outpost stations, wardrobe items, familiar companions, and audio/visual preferences are stored locally on your device using standard local storage and Android application preferences.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection or Sharing

The app does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal or sensitive user data—including your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera feed, microphone audio, precise or approximate location, or Advertising ID.

3. Zero Advertisements & Zero In-App Purchases

  • No Third-Party Ad SDKs: Potion & Pawn: Idle Outpost contains zero advertisements (no banner ads, no interstitial ads, and no rewarded video ads) and embeds no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No Microtransactions: All Supply Chests, Moonstone Runes, Wardrobe Gear, and Familiar Companions are earned exclusively through normal in-game progression with zero real-money microtransactions. Your upfront Google Play purchase is processed directly by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Potion & Pawn: Idle Outpost does not collect personal information from any user, including children.

5. Managing or Deleting Your Local Save Data

You can export or import a local save backup string or wipe your local progress at any time inside the in-game Settings → Reset Save Data menu, or uninstall the application to remove all locally stored game data from your device.
