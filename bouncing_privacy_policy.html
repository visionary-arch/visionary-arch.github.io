Privacy Policy — Bouncing Idle: Brick Breaker

Zero Ads Zero Microtransactions No Personal Data Collected 100% Offline Playable

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

1. Overview

Bouncing Idle: Brick Breaker (com.bouncingidlebrickbreaker.game) is a premium, paid-upfront offline arcade idle game. We respect your privacy and designed this application so that it collects, transmits, sells, or shares zero personal data.

2. Information Collection & Use

  • No Personal Information: The game does not ask for, access, or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: The application contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, crash trackers, or behavioral profiling libraries.
  • No Advertising or In-App Purchases: There are zero advertisements, zero Advertising ID queries, and zero real-money microtransactions inside the game.

3. Local Save Data & Android Auto-Backup

Your game progress, settings, achievements, and lifetime statistics are stored locally on your device using standard app storage (WebView localStorage and Android SharedPreferences). If you have Android System Auto-Backup enabled on your Google account, Android may back up your local game save to your personal Google Drive app-data folder so your progress restores if you switch phones. The developer never has access to your Google account or backup files.

You may also manually export or import your save string in Settings, or erase all local save data at any time using the Hard Reset option inside Settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

Because Bouncing Idle: Brick Breaker collects zero personal or device information from any user of any age, it complies with COPPA, GDPR, and Google Play Families / General Audience data standards.

5. Changes to This Policy

If future updates ever change how local data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated inside the application and on our store listing page prior to release.
