Privacy Policy — Storybook Idle Incremental

Package ID: com.storybookidle.game • Effective Date: November 18, 2026

1. Offline Local Progress & Zero Account Tracking

Storybook Idle Incremental works offline without requiring an account or sign-in. Your Golden Ink, 12 chapter lines, Bookmark upgrades, Companion Scouts, and storybook progress are stored locally on your device (localStorage and Android SharedPreferences). You may export a save backup or reset local progress at any time in Story & Shop → Save Data & Backup.

2. Personal Information

The developer does not collect, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera feed, microphone audio, or precise location.

3. In-App Purchases & Opt-In Rewarded Ads

  • Zero Forced Ads: Storybook Idle Incremental never interrupts gameplay with forced interstitial or banner advertisements.
  • Google Play Billing: Optional one-time unlocks (Double All Cascade Output, 2nd Scout Slot) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google Play.
  • Optional Rewarded Ads: Choosing to watch an optional rewarded video ad uses Google AdMob under Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) regional consent rules, which may process standard advertising identifiers, coarse IP region, and ad diagnostics.

4. Children's Privacy

Storybook Idle Incremental is intended for general teen and adult audiences (13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
