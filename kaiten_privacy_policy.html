Privacy Policy - Kaiten Express: Idle Sushi Bar

Package ID: com.kaitenexpress.idlesushi • Effective Date: November 27, 2026

1. Offline Local Progress and Zero Account Tracking

Kaiten Express: Idle Sushi Bar works offline without requiring an account or sign-in. Your Venues, Dishes, Staff Upgrades, Test Kitchen Research, Chef Star Upgrades, Prize Shelf Collection, and Trophies are stored locally on your device (localStorage and Android SharedPreferences). You can export a save backup or reset local progress at any time in Settings.

2. Personal Information

The developer does not collect, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera feed, microphone audio, or precise location.

3. In-App Purchases and Opt-In Rewarded Ads

  • Zero Forced Ads: Kaiten Express: Idle Sushi Bar never interrupts gameplay with forced interstitial or banner advertisements.
  • Google Play Billing: The optional one-time in-app purchase (Remove Voluntary Ads Pass) is processed securely through Google Play Billing, which records Purchase history for app functionality and fraud prevention. Payment details are handled by Google Play.
  • Optional Rewarded Ads: Choosing to watch an optional rewarded video ad uses Google AdMob under Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) regional consent rules. When enabled, Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share Device or other IDs (including Advertising ID), Approximate location inferred from IP address, App interactions, Crash logs, and Diagnostics for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Learn more at How Google uses data when you use partner apps.

4. Children's Privacy

Kaiten Express: Idle Sushi Bar is intended for general audiences aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
