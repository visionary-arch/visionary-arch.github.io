Privacy Policy - Shaftworks: Idle Mine

Package ID: com.shaftworksidlemine.game • Effective Date: November 22, 2026

1. Offline Local Progress & Zero Account Tracking

Shaftworks: Idle Mine works offline without requiring an account or sign-in. Your Worlds, Mines, Managers, Relics, Pets, Research, and achievements are stored locally on your device (localStorage and Android SharedPreferences). You can export a save backup or reset local progress at any time in Shop → Settings.

2. Personal Information

The developer does not collect, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera feed, microphone audio, or precise location.

3. In-App Purchases & Opt-In Rewarded Ads

  • Zero Forced Ads: Shaftworks: Idle Mine never interrupts gameplay with forced interstitial or banner advertisements.
  • Google Play Billing: Optional in-app purchases (Ad-Free VIP Pass, x3 Ad Earnings Upgrade, 2nd & 3rd Expedition Slots, and Vault of Gems) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google Play.
  • Optional Rewarded Ads: Choosing to watch an optional rewarded video ad uses Google AdMob under Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) regional consent rules, which may process standard advertising identifiers, coarse IP region, crash logs, diagnostics, and ad interaction metrics.

4. Children's Privacy

Shaftworks: Idle Mine is intended for general teen and adult audiences (13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
