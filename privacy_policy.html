Privacy Policy — Pinfall: Idle Drop

Package ID: com.pinfallidledrop.game • Effective Date: November 2, 2026

1. Local Game Progress & Data Storage

Pinfall: Idle Drop stores your game progress, cabinet settings, and unlocked items locally on your device using standard local storage. You may export a manual backup code or wipe your local save at any time inside Cabinet Settings.

2. Personal Information

The developer does not directly collect, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera feed, microphone audio, or precise location.

3. In-App Purchases & Optional Rewarded Ads

  • Zero Forced Ads: Pinfall: Idle Drop never displays unprompted or forced interstitial ads.
  • Google Play Billing: Optional one-time feature licenses and token packs are processed securely by Google Play. Payment credentials are handled exclusively by Google Play and are never shared with the app.
  • Voluntary Rewarded Boosts: When you choose to watch an optional rewarded video ad (for example, Speed Dynamo, Revenue Dynamo, 2× Offline Coins, or 20-Minute Auto-Motor), the ad provider (such as Google AdMob) may process standard device identifiers, coarse IP region, and ad-interaction diagnostics in accordance with your regional consent preferences and Google's Privacy Policy. Purchasing Remove All Voluntary Ads disables ad requests across all voluntary boosters.

4. Children's Privacy

Pinfall: Idle Drop does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Your Controls

You can reset your local save data inside Cabinet Settings → Factory Hard Reset, or uninstall the application at any time to remove all locally stored game data from your device.
