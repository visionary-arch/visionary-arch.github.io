Privacy Policy: Rainspill: Idle Storm

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · Contact: marieodonnell@usa.com

1. Local Save Data & Photo Album Storage

Rainspill: Idle Storm stores your game progress, audio and display settings, Daily Journal stamps, and saved valley snapshots locally on your device using browser storage, IndexedDB, and app backup storage on mobile builds. All screenshots remain on your device unless you share or download them yourself. You can export a backup code or clear your local progress inside Settings at any time.

2. Audience & Personal Information

Rainspill: Idle Storm is intended for players aged 13 and older. The developer does not ask for, store, or sell your name, physical camera feed, microphone audio, contacts, or precise GPS location. Photo Album snapshots are captured directly from the game scene inside the app.

3. Voluntary Rewarded Ads & Google Play Purchases

  • No Forced Ads: The game contains zero forced or unprompted video ads. Watching the single +50% Raindrop Boost rewarded ad is always your choice.
  • Google Play Purchases: In app purchases (36 Hour Offline Rain, Sky & Atmosphere Pack, Rare Wildlife Coat Pack, Studio Photo Pack, and Ad Free Rain Boost Pass) and purchase restorations are processed directly through Google Play Billing under Google privacy policies.
  • SDK Data Safety Categories: On Android releases where Google AdMob and Google Play Billing operate, Google SDKs may process the following standard data types required for ad delivery, purchase verification, fraud prevention, and app stability: Approximate location, Purchase history, App interactions, Crash logs, Diagnostics, and Device or other IDs.
  • Ad Privacy Choices: Where regional privacy laws apply, a consent prompt is shown before rewarded ads load, and you can update or withdraw your consent at any time using the Ad Privacy Options button inside Settings.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

You can delete your local save progress and saved Album photos at any time using Hard Reset inside Settings or by clearing app storage in your device settings. For any privacy questions, contact marieodonnell@usa.com.
