Privacy Policy — Marble Loop: ASMR Merge Idle

Package ID: com.marbleloopidle.game • Effective Date: November 7, 2026

1. Local Save Data & Android Auto-Backup

Marble Loop: ASMR Merge Idle stores gameplay progress, glass marble tiers, wooden board upgrades, and audio/display preferences locally on your device. On Android, your save state is mirrored to scoped application storage (marble_loop_backup_prefs) so Android Auto-Backup and device transfer can preserve your progress if enabled in your Android system settings. No external game account or sign-up is required.

2. Personal Information

This application does not directly collect, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera feed, microphone audio, or precise GPS location.

3. Optional Rewarded Video Ads & Ad Privacy (Google AdMob & UMP)

  • Opt-In Rewarded Video Only: The game contains zero forced interstitial or banner ads. Rewarded video ads load and play only when you choose to watch an optional 11-minute Coin Boost (2x Coins by default, or 3x Coins if the optional 3x Ad Boost Upgrade is owned) or an optional daily +18 Glass Gems & +3 Golden Gears video (up to 3 per day) inside the Coin Boost & Daily Gems popup.
  • Identifiers & Diagnostics: Optional rewarded video ads are served through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Depending on your device settings and regional consent choices, Google AdMob may collect or process the Android Advertising ID, coarse IP region, device diagnostics, and ad-interaction events to deliver ads and prevent invalid traffic, governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Consent Management: Players in applicable jurisdictions (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and eligible US states) are presented with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent choices and may review or update their ad privacy preferences at any time via Settings → Ad Privacy Choices, or reset/delete the Advertising ID in Android system settings.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional one-time purchases — the 3x Ad Boost Upgrade (ad_boost_3x_upgrade_299) and the paid-only 3D Cute Helper Monkey (golden_monkey_helper_499) — are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. Payment card and billing credentials are handled by Google Play and are never transmitted to or stored by this application. Ownership status is verified through Google Play so purchases can be restored across devices signed into the same Google account via the Coin Boost & Daily Gems popup or Settings → Restore.

5. Audience & Children’s Privacy

Marble Loop: ASMR Merge Idle is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

6. Data Controls & Deletion

You may export a Base64 save backup at any time via Settings → Export Save Code, erase all local and mirrored save progress via Settings → Hard Reset Save, or uninstall the application to remove all local application data from your device.

7. Contact & Support

For privacy inquiries or support regarding Marble Loop: ASMR Merge Idle, please use the Developer Support email address provided on the official Marble Loop: ASMR Merge Idle Google Play Store listing page.
